Case study / GoHighLevel × Make × ServiceTitan

From form fill to booked job

An end-to-end lead pipeline for HVAC contractors: qualify in GoHighLevel, book on the calendar, build the job in ServiceTitan, and keep both systems in sync.

  1. Form
  2. Calendar
  3. GHL
  4. Make
  5. ServiceTitan
  6. Sync
  1. 01

    Branching intake

    An eight-step funnel sorts every lead before a human touches it (install vs. service, AC vs. furnace, estimate or not), and each path triggers its own automation.

  2. 02

    Straight onto the calendar

    The final step is a live calendar with three-hour arrival windows (evenings included). Picking a slot creates the appointment. No phone tag.

  3. 03

    Tag, enroll, hand off

    The booking fires a GHL workflow that tags the contact and ends in a webhook passing the payload to Make.com.

  4. 04

    38 modules build the job

    The Make scenario matches the customer, assembles the payload and creates the job in ServiceTitan, routing install, service and maintenance separately.

  5. 05

    Status flows home

    A scheduled scenario polls ServiceTitan for job and invoice state and updates the GHL opportunity, so the pipeline always reflects reality.

  6. 06

    Self-healing auth

    ServiceTitan tokens expire by design; a small scenario refreshes and stores them on a schedule so nobody ever has to think about it.

New leads

TCPA consent decides the cadence: SMS + email for opted-in leads, manual tasks where consent is missing.

Follow up

Stage changes re-enroll the contact; six texts and three emails go out until someone replies.

Appointments

Fans out by service category, finds or creates the opportunity, then sends confirmation, 24-hour and 1-hour reminders.
